I am a developer and writer interested in the craft of building for the web. Most of my work sits somewhere between type systems, interface design, and the performance budget.

This blog is where I collect the ideas that survive a second draft. Some posts are practical walkthroughs; others are notes on decisions that took longer than expected.

## What I care about

- Software that is easy to change, not just easy to demo.
- Interfaces that work with a keyboard, a screen reader, and a slow connection.
- Writing that respects the reader’s time.
- Owning the tools and formats I depend on.
